Break down [PROCESS] into 4–6 simple visual steps.

Requirements:
- Each step should be immediately understandable at a glance.
- Use minimal text.
- Prioritize visual clarity over decoration.
- Keep the composition clean and organized.
- Use consistent icons, colors, and layout across all steps.
- Make each step visually distinct.
- Include arrows, spacing, or visual flow indicators where helpful.
- Avoid clutter and unnecessary details.
- Design in a modern infographic style.
- Ensure the final output is readable on both desktop and mobile.
