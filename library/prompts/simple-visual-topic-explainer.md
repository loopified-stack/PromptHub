Explain [TOPIC] in the simplest possible way.

Requirements:
- Assume I have absolutely no prior knowledge.
- Use plain, everyday language.
- Avoid jargon unless absolutely necessary.
- If a technical term must be used, explain it immediately in simple words.
- Use visual metaphors, analogies, or simple imaginary illustrations to make concepts intuitive.
- Break the explanation into small, easy-to-follow sections.
- Prioritize clarity over completeness.
- Make the explanation feel conversational and beginner-friendly.
- Use examples from daily life whenever possible.
- End with a short one-sentence summary that captures the core idea.
